The tour

Inside the platform.

What each part of Cinebody actually does, in the order you'll use it, shown with real screens from the product, web and app. Click any screen to look closer.

Pulse

See everything happening, live

One screen with the whole operation: where clips are being filmed right now, how quality is trending, which projects need you, and every filmer conversation. On your desk or in your pocket.

Pulse · web and app
Projects & Idea Studio

From idea to plan in a minute

Stuck? Idea Studio deals you shoot ideas built on your brand; keep one and it becomes a project. Then the wizard writes the shot list from your description: every shot with plain directions, orientations, even slow-mo flags. Edit anything, invite filmers, done.

An idea becomes a plan · dealt, generated, editable
AI Director

Every filmer gets a director

The camera opens with the shot's brief right on screen, and the AI checks framing, light, focus, steadiness, and audio live while they film. When every check clears, they nailed it. First-take footage from anyone.

The real capture screen · brief, then live checks
AI Voice Interviews

An interviewer in every pocket

For story shots, the AI interviews people out loud, listens to the answer, and asks the follow-up a producer would. Real stories pulled out of anyone, on any phone, in any city.

AI Voice Interview · it asks, listens, and follows up
Messages, Directions & Map

Run the shoot from your desk

Watch clips land on the live map as people film. Message one filmer or all of them, or send a direction mid-shoot: it hits their phone as a notification while the camera is up. Course-correct in the moment, not after the footage is home.

Directions land as notifications, while they film
Library & AI Clip Review

Footage that grades itself

Every clip lands transcribed and scored: audio, stability, focus, exposure, framing, plus a plain verdict and what would make it better. Open any clip for the full review, and search everything by what people actually said.

AI Clip Review · web and app
Payouts

Incentivize your best filmers

The best footage deserves real money behind it: flat rates, per-clip bounties, or gift cards, your call per project. Filmers see what it pays before they film, connect Stripe once in the app, and the money lands the moment you approve.

Payouts · you fund it, filmers cash out in the app
Post Production

From a pile of clips to a timeline in one click

It reads all your footage, tells you the story it found, and one click builds the cut: best takes in order, B-roll over the seams, captions, music. Don't love it? Type a note like "get to the hook faster" and it revises just that.

Auto-Editor · the brief, then the cut
Brand Kit

Your brand, learned once

Point it at your website and it drafts your logo, colors, voice, and filming rules. From then on, every shot list, cover, caption, and cut stays on brand automatically.

Brand Kit · both pages, fetched from your site

Run a global shoot without leaving your desk.

One brief, filmers anywhere, all on-brand, all organized, footage back in real time. No crews flown in, no logistics, no lost weekends.
