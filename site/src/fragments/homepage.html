The connected pipeline

One system runs your whole production.

A connected pipeline where every step feeds the next: shotlist, coaching, scoring, edit. Everything a producer, a director, and an editor used to own, running as one system.

Stage 01

AI Director

Give it your goal and it writes the shotlist, then coaches every person on camera, live: framing, light, steadiness, even whether the location works. First-take pro footage from anyone.

AI Director · live on-camera coaching
Stage 02

Clip scoring

Every clip is transcribed and scored the second it lands: light, focus, framing, audio, delivery. The best moments rise to the top and weak takes get flagged on the spot, so you review the best, not the whole pile.

AI clip review · scored as it lands
Stage 03

The auto-editor

It reads the transcripts, finds the real story in what people actually said, and cuts a finished, on-brand video: paced to music, captioned, reframed for vertical. You open a video that is nearly done.
