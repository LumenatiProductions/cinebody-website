Who We Are

The platform, and the team, behind three years of fleet content.

Cinebody is a patented video capture platform that lets brands collect professional-quality footage from their own people: no production crews flown in, no outsourced talent. The platform can guide capture directly with a shot list controlling resolution, framing, and creative direction. Royal Caribbean's onboard crew capture on their own phones and use the platform primarily to organize and store that footage, which syncs to the dashboard the moment it's recorded. From there, Cinebody's in-house creative services team, producers and editors who already know the brand, turn raw footage into finished, on-brand content, so Royal Caribbean's team never touches a timeline. Since August 2023, that combination has run as a dedicated content engine for the fleet.
