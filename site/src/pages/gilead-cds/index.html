The mechanics

How it works. No surprises.

01

Length follows the edit

Pricing is based on the finished video's length, not headcount or how many people are on camera. A typical CDS video runs about a minute and a half, and the subscription supports videos up to roughly three minutes.

02

Full production support included

Shot lists, filmer onboarding, and monitoring footage as it comes in are all part of the service, not an add-on.

03

You approve before anything ships

We script and plan around what your team wants, send it for approval, then send it to your employees to film.

04

Two rounds of revisions, built in

Every video includes two rounds of timecoded revisions before final delivery.
