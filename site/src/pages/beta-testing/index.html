Setup

Get set up

Two things to install: the web platform, and the mobile app. Takes about five minutes.

Web platform

Head to cinebody.vercel.app and create an account with whichever email you'd like tied to your organization.

This is where briefs, projects, footage, and payouts live: the control room for everything below.

Mobile app, via TestFlight

Travis on our team will send you an invite via TestFlight to download the beta iOS app, using the Apple ID email we already have on file for you.

You'll get an email from Apple's TestFlight. If you don't already have the TestFlight app, grab it from the App Store first, then open our invite from that same device to install Cinebody.

No Android app yet. We're working on it, but it isn't ready for external testing just yet. For now, please stick to the iOS app or film directly from your browser's camera using a project link. No install required for that path.
What's ready

What's ready to test

Not a checklist. Just the parts of this release we're proudest of, and the ones most worth exploring.

Idea generator

Start before you have a brief. Surface concepts and directions you can shape into a real project.

AI shot-list generator

Turn a brief into a full, directable shot list in seconds. Reorder, cut, or rewrite any shot yourself.

AI director

Walks a filmer through the shot list in the app, one shot at a time, so nobody needs a script to follow.

AI Interviewer

Send a filmer an on-camera interview they record solo. No crew, no scheduling, questions asked for you.

Simple filmer links

Send anyone a link and they can film for a project with zero login. The real path most one-off filmers will actually use.

Footage scoring & best takes

Upload footage and watch it get scored on the way in, with the strongest takes surfaced automatically.

Batch download

Pull every clip from a project at once, ready for your own editor or archive.

Brand kit

Point it at your own site and it'll pull your logo and colors in automatically. No manual upload required.

Payouts

Fund an incentive on a project and watch a filmer cash out. It's all sandboxed in Stripe's test mode, so nothing here touches a real card or bank account.

Testing Payouts: funding card 4000 0000 0000 0077, any future expiry, any CVC, any ZIP. On the filmer's side, use any made-up SSN, phone number, past birthdate, and address. It all auto-verifies in test mode.
Coming next: the AI editor, which will assemble a finished cut from your best takes automatically. It's still in training and isn't ready to test yet, but it's what we're building toward, and you'll be invited back to try it.
Known issues

Where things stand

This is a beta. Most of this is already fixed or actively being worked on. Nothing here should block you, just don't be surprised.

  • Sign-in with Google or Apple isn't wired up yet on the login page. Email and password work fine.
  • Open capture mode doesn't always show a clear "you're done" confirmation once every shot is finished. The footage is still there, the checkmark is just quiet.
  • Filming from a browser link on iPhone Safari. The way back to your shot list can be hard to spot, especially in landscape.
  • Interview mode may write something into the transcript even if a question went unanswered in silence. Being fixed.
  • Sign-up emails can be a little slow to land if a lot of people are joining at once. Give it a few minutes before assuming it's stuck.

This is the single most useful thing you can do for us during this round.

01

02
