This is the raw layer that feeds it.

This complements that work, keeping organic and paid social active while the studio captures the bigger endeavors. Concept 01 of 04
Fan Journey Vignettes

Three to five season ticket holders. One gameday each. Start to finish.

Recruit season ticket holders per game and capture their full gameday arc: the jersey going on at home, the drive in, the tailgate, the walk into CPKC, the noise. Cinebody builds the shot list and directs remotely; they film on the phone already in their pocket. Sideline passes, autographed gear, or a next-game VIP upgrade for the featured fans keep quality high. No crews, no camera-shy strangers, no staged moments.

What you get
  • Individual hero vignettes per fan, story-driven and intimate
  • 24-hour quick-turn recap compiling every fan's game
  • Evergreen b-roll bank of rituals, tailgate, and atmosphere for next season's promo cycle

Concept 02 of 04
Fan-Sourced UGC Contest

Put your own audience to work. A shot list, a VIP prize, a flood of content.

A CTA across KC Current's own channels. Fans film to a Cinebody-built shot list (pregame rituals, the chant, trick shots, pride content) for a shot at a VIP playoff experience. It's fan-as-hero by design: the people who already show up become the ones making the content, and the entry mechanic itself drives engagement before a single asset is cut.

What you get
  • High-volume raw UGC feed for KC Current's channels, through the playoffs and into next season
  • Quick-turn hype compilation that's democratized, high-energy, and fan-as-hero
  • The winner's VIP experience documented as a follow-up piece

Concept 03 of 04
Current Landing Experience Series

Current Landing is a destination. Nobody's filmed it like one.

Document the place, not just the match. A creator or an employee takes a full day at Current Landing end to end: arrival, the shops, the food, the studio, the walk into CPKC Stadium, into the game. Creator-led keeps quality and timing controllable, rather than depending on fan availability the way Concept 01 does.

Why it fits
  • Proves the full-day case. A KC Current game is a destination, not a 90-minute product
  • Evergreen and year-round. Works for ticket sales and tourism, not just the playoff window
  • Opens a co-marketing door. Naturally features Current Landing businesses, which may create sponsorship angles

Concept 04 of 04
Player Insight Series

Recurring player content, scoped to whatever access you can unlock.

Lightweight and repeatable. Cadence stays flexible (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) and the format scales down or up depending on how much player time is realistically available. Nothing here requires a shoot day.

Format options, low to high lift
  • Rapid-fire Q&A. Proven format, high shareability, minutes of player time
  • Day-in-the-life / training snippets. More access, more texture
  • Players reacting to fan UGC from Concept 02, tying the whole fan campaign back to the team
Note: Unlike the other concepts, which are more fan and creator-driven, this one involves team and comms buy-in for participation.
