The full AI production loop

Brand rules write the shoot. Models understand footage as it lands. One clock locks the cut. Human taste compounds into the next brief.

Four beats. One return path. The hub is Brand Kit + word clock + recipe.
The bet

Production AI should run during the shoot and the land, not as a black box after the bin is full.

Most tools optimize the last mile. Cinebody wires Brand Kit → coach → grade → word clock → paper edit → graphics → learn-back so every take teaches the next one.
Typical
Linear. Blind until the bin is full. No shared clock.
Cinebody
Closed loop. Understands as it lands. Taste feeds back.
Three innovations

Brand Kit is the recipe

Voice, look, and rules aren’t a PDF on a shelf. Every later model inherits them.

ONE word clock locks the cut

“Clock lock” = shared Scribe syllables. Timeline, captions, and graphics snap to the same words.

Learn-back compounds

Human finals distill into shotlist rules, paper-edit library, graphics taste, and filmer Pulse.
