## The pipeline (the star) — "Follow one shoot through the whole pipeline"
- Intro: Watch a single brief become a finished video, stage by stage.
1. **The brief becomes a shotlist.** You give it the goal; it writes the exact shots to get, so everyone films with a plan, not a guess.
2. **The AI Director coaches every shot, live.** Framing, light, steadiness, in real time, and it knows a bad location from a good one. First-take pro footage from anyone. *(img04/img05)*
3. **Every clip is scored as it lands.** Light, focus, framing, audio, delivery, so the best moments surface and weak takes get flagged on the spot. *(img08)*
4. **The auto-editor finds the story and cuts it.** It reads the transcripts, finds the real narrative in what people said, and assembles a finished cut: paced, captioned, reframed for vertical. *(img09)*
5. **You open a finished video.** Not a folder of clips. Ready to publish, or hand to your team to polish. *(img00/img01/img02)*
- Closer: **Real people create it. AI runs it end to end.**
