Continuous content engine

Royal Caribbean needed a constant stream of authentic content from ships across their entire fleet. Flying production crews to every vessel wasn't scalable -- and the content always felt staged.

With Cinebody deployed fleet-wide, crew members now capture on-brand content using guided shot lists -- entertainment, dining, port excursions, behind-the-scenes moments. No travel budgets, no scheduling conflicts, no third-party crews. Just an always-on content engine powered by the people who know the ships best.

The Challenge

Dozens of ships, constant itineraries, impossible to staff with traditional production. Content felt polished but inauthentic.

The Result

An always-on content pipeline from the people who live and work on the ships. Authentic, scalable, and 100% on brand.
