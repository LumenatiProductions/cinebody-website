# Cinebody Site — Design Influence Board

For the rebuild. Target feel: dark, cinematic, real footage as the hero, bold confident type, human and crafted (NOT the cold AI-gen gradient look, since we're the anti-slop brand), with product-UI shown proudly.

---

## The core references (real sites)

### Video-forward / cinematic (the spine of our look)
- **Frame.io** — https://frame.io — dark, footage-forward, real-production feel. Closest to our "real video is the hero" instinct. Study how footage IS the UI.
- **Runway** — https://runwayml.com — cinematic, editorial, film-frame sections, one strong typeface. Borrow the *craft and cinematic restraint*, NOT the AI-generated aesthetic (that's the slop pole we position against).
- **Mux** — https://mux.com — clean video-infra premium. We literally run on Mux; worth seeing how they present video simply.

### Product-marketing polish (how to present the pipeline + features)
- **Linear** — https://linear.app — the gold standard. Dark, crisp, restrained, exceptional feature sections with real product-UI shots. Best model for the Platform page pipeline walkthrough.
- **Vercel** — https://vercel.com — bold dark, premium, product-forward.
- **Stripe** — https://stripe.com — clarity + polish benchmark; how to make a complex product feel simple.

### Bold / human / editorial (the "real people" soul + anti-slop warmth)
- **Ramp** — https://ramp.com — confident type, bold, businesslike but alive.
- **Arc (The Browser Company)** — https://arc.net — human, playful-premium, personality.
- **Framer** — https://framer.com — motion-rich, kinetic type (for how the site *moves*).

### Authentic-brand energy (tone/imagery for real-people content)
- **Nike** campaign sites + **Aerie** (the brand that went publicly anti-AI, from our research) — for the human, real-people energy the footage should carry.

## The synthesis for Cinebody
Frame.io's footage-forward darkness + Linear's product-feature precision + Ramp/Arc's human boldness, with Runway's cinematic craft but none of its AI-generated coldness. The site itself should feel made by real people, because that's the whole pitch.
